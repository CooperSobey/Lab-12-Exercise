Q3. What happens to the bounding boxes with update + fullRefit?
    When we call full refit on the bvh, our bounding boxes update to move with the balls.
    As in, as the balls move the bounding box both resizes to fit if multiple balls are moving
    in different directions, or follows the ball as its edge is pushed in the direction the ball
    is moving.

Q4. What happens with reset (rebuild every frame) instead?
    With reset, as the balls move, the bounding boxes are redrawn around the balls.
    Unlike with rebuild that resized the bounding boxes but always held/were affected by the same balls,
    reset redraws the boxes causing different balls to be grouped into one bounding box and the boxes are constantly
    changing rather than just growing, shrinking, and moving. This is because the parent-child pairing change and the bounding boxes change with that.

Q5. Why are some circles drawn with their sides cut off?
    While I couldn't see the circles drawn with their sides cut off myself, my guess is that its because if a ball crosses
    a bounding box before the box properly updates around it, the part of the ball that crossed doesn't get drawn as its seen
    as !(*this & other).empty() is true.
